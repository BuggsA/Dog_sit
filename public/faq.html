FAQ

Questions, answered

Everything you might want to know about walks, drop-ins and overnights with me. Don't see your question? Just text me at (541) 801-1068.

Services

What services do you offer?

Dog walks and drop-in visits (15, 30 or 60 minutes), overnight pet sitting and house sitting, and care for cats and small pets. See all services.

What kinds of pets do you care for?

Dogs are my specialty, and I happily care for cats, birds, guinea pigs and turtles too. If you have another kind of pet, just ask.

What's included in every visit?

Photo updates every visit, videos on request, fresh food and water, playtime and cuddles, scooped litter boxes, trash taken out, plants watered, and an eye on the house.

Can you give my pet medication?

Yes, and I'm very comfortable doing it. I've given lots of pets their medication, so just tell me what your pet takes and when, and I'll make sure they get it. Medication is part of walks and drop-in visits, and of cat and small-pet care. If anything ever seems off, I'll call your vet and let you know right away.

How long are you away from my pets during an overnight?

Never more than 5 hours at a time. The 10, 12, 20 or 24 hours you choose are the time I guarantee I'll be at home with your pets each day, and I'm usually there even longer. I might head out for other walks or a quick stop at home, but I'm always back within 5 hours.

Will I get updates while I'm away?

Yes. You get a photo update after every visit, and at least two a day during overnights. Videos whenever you'd like.

I'm nervous about leaving my dog with someone. How will I know they're OK?

I completely understand. I know how stressful it can be to leave your dog with someone, whether you're at work for the day or away on vacation. My goal is to put your mind at ease. You'll get a photo update after every visit, videos whenever you'd like, and it's always me who comes, so your dog sees a familiar face. Text me anytime to check in.

Pricing

How much does a dog walk cost?

Visits are $10 for 15 minutes, $17 for 30 minutes and $30 for an hour. Try the price estimator.

How much is overnight pet sitting?

Per night, based on the hours I guarantee at home with your pets each day: $50 for at least 10 hours, $60 for at least 12 hours, $80 for at least 20 hours and $120 for 24 hours. Whichever you choose, I'm never away for more than 5 hours at a time.

Is there a minimum visit length?

Visits that include feeding are at least 30 minutes, and so are all visits for cats and small pets.

Do puppies or senior dogs cost more?

Dogs under 1 year or over 14 need a little extra care, so their pricing is custom. Just text me at (541) 801-1068 for a quote.

Are there booking fees?

No. There are no booking fees, and photo updates are always included.

What about holidays?

Holiday dates fill up quickly. Please ask in advance about holiday availability and pricing.

Service area

What areas do you serve?

I'm based in Reston. Walks and drop-ins cover about a 20-minute drive from Reston. Overnights cover Fairfax County and eastern Loudoun County, out to Aldie. Check your ZIP code.

Do you pet sit in Washington, DC?

Yes, overnights only, by request, at a 50% upcharge.

My ZIP code is outside your area. Can you still help?

Maybe! Text me at (541) 801-1068 and I'll let you know.

Booking

How do I book?

Send a request on the booking page with your dates and services. It goes straight to my phone and I'll reply to confirm.

Do we meet before the first visit?

Yes. New clients start with a meet & greet so I can meet your pets, learn their routine and pick up keys. It's free in Fairfax County and $10 in Loudoun County.

Who will actually care for my pets?

Me, every time. Caring for pets has been my full-time job since 2019. It's not a side gig or an app, so your pets get one familiar face they can trust.

How far ahead should I book?

As soon as you know your plans, especially for holidays, which book up early.

Still have a question?

Text me anytime. I'm happy to help you figure out what your pets need.
