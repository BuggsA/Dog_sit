Why older dogs

It started with Lucy

Lucy was twelve when she came home with me. A lot of people would have looked at a twelve-year-old dog and thought her best years were behind her. Lucy lived another five and a half years, and they were full of sniffing, jumping and chasing squirrels.

She taught me that older dogs have so much love left to give. Too many of them end up without a home, often through no fault of their own, and they're usually the last to be adopted.

What I'm working toward

My dream is to open a pet hotel or a rescue for older dogs. I want it to be a safe home for the dogs who can't find one: somewhere calm and warm, where they're never rushed on a walk and always have a lap nearby.

I'm not there yet. But every walk, every drop-in and every overnight I do for your pets gets me one step closer.
