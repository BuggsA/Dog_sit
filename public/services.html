Services

Care that fits your pet's routine

Whether it's a midday walk or a week-long trip, your pets stay comfortable at home with someone who knows them.

Dog walks & drop-in visits

Perfect for long workdays, puppies who need a midday break, or seniors who need a little extra help.

  • 15 minutes: a quick potty break and a cuddle
  • 30 minutes: a walk plus feeding (the minimum when meals are included)
  • 1 hour: a long adventure walk with plenty of play

Available within about a 20-minute drive of Reston.

Overnights & house sitting

I stay in your home so your pets keep their own beds, routines and favorite spots, and your house never looks empty.

  • Choose 10, 12, 20 or 24 hours with your pets each day
  • Never away from your pets for more than 5 hours at a time
  • At least two photo updates a day
  • Trash, plants and an eye on the house

Across Fairfax County and eastern Loudoun County, out to Aldie. Washington, DC by request (+50%).

Cats & small pets

Cats, birds, guinea pigs and turtles get the same love and attention as the dogs.

  • Visits are 30 minutes minimum, so there's real time to play
  • Fresh food and water, medication if needed
  • Litter boxes scooped, cages and tanks tidied
  • Photo update every visit
