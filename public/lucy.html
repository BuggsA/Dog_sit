Her story

A promise kept

My pet-sitting journey started in Salt Lake City, and so did my journey with Lucy.

Lucy's first mom and I had an agreement: if anything ever happened to her, I would take care of Lucy. When she passed away from COVID, I kept that promise. Lucy was twelve years old when she came home with me.

Most people don't expect a twelve-year-old dog to have many adventures left. Lucy didn't care what people expected. She lived another five and a half years and came along for every step, from Utah all the way to Virginia, after a life that had already taken her through California.

Stubborn, curious, and completely herself

Lucy was not in a hurry. On walks she would stop every few steps, dig her feet in, and refuse to move until she had smelled everything: every blade of grass, every fence post, every leaf. You didn't walk Lucy; you went on a sniffing expedition with her.

She was also a jumper. Even as a senior, she could leap several feet into the air, and she loved doing it.

We took care of each other

People think of it as me taking care of Lucy, but she took care of me just as much. Through hard times and happy ones, she was right there. I like to think we got each other through.

What Lucy taught me

She still shapes every visit

Walks for Lucy is named for her because the way I care for your pets comes straight from the years I spent with her.

A walk is for sniffing

Lucy taught me patience. Your dog's walk is about them, so there's always time to stop and smell things.

Senior dogs have so much left

Lucy came to me at twelve and lived to seventeen and a half. I know how to care for older dogs, and how much joy they still have to give.

A promise is a promise

When you trust me with your pet and your home, I take it as seriously as the promise I made for Lucy.
